Will Weathersbee Automated Assistant

An automated AI assistant service operated by sole proprietor Will Weathersbee. Invited users text questions and the automated assistant replies with information such as schedules, weather, lookups, and relayed messages, and sends reminders users ask for.

How to opt in

There are three ways to opt in. Every way ends with you texting START to (854) 254-5107 from your own phone.

  1. Online: Sign up with the web form. Enter your mobile number, check the consent box, and tap Sign up. Your messaging app opens a text that says START; send it to confirm.
  2. By text: Text START to (854) 254-5107 to use the automated assistant. You will receive a confirmation text.
  3. In person: Will Weathersbee may invite you in person by reading this script: “Would you like to get texts from Will Weathersbee Automated Assistant at (854) 254-5107? It sends replies to your texts, schedule info, messages relayed for me, and reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. If you agree, text START to (854) 254-5107 to confirm.” You are only opted in after you text START.

Message frequency varies based on how often you text. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

The assistant never sends marketing or promotional messages and never messages anyone who has not texted it first.
